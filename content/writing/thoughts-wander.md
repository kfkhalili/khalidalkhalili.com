The trainer speaks not to me,  
but at me.  
The complex matters of  
Architecture and Design.

Sitting on a bench, a lone  
tree in a field,  
the clouds pass us by.  
Our minds intertwined

A discordant cacophony  
of noises cast  
Blur into the background  
and fade away

And I find myself longing  
to be lost  
In the meadows of your  
embrace.
