On limber legs she dances.

Her presentation is, at least to her, a mode of propitiation. A self-contained enactment, capable of soothing an angry monarch or consoling a mournful god. This is her prayer and her fast. Her meditation.

The accompanying music plays softly, augmenting her every movement and serving in the capacity of a partner that leads her from one fluid stance to another. Transforming her from an amateur, to an expert, to a virtuoso.

A spotlight from an unknown location falls on our sole performer. It casts an illumination on the ethereal fabric that covers her agile body. The gauzy material flutters lightly with every gentle hop, embellishing her every move while giving onlookers an impression of weightlessness.

As the audience becomes captivated by the elegant performance, observation turns into obsession. The dancer ascends the slope of ingenuity by executing a series of complex moves that further dazzle the crowd and leave them awestruck, disallowing her to leave their sights even for a moment, lest they miss an imagined finish or a signature flourish.

The light hits her body gently and casts a shadow behind her. The intensity of light, the darkness of the surrounding stands, and the vigor, vitality, and skill of the performer imbue the shadow with a fraction of the expended energy. A minute life of its own, leased to it for the duration of the concert.

The silhouette dances, rushing to mimic the performer. It moves with a relentless tenacity and an alacrity to perform, to earn the respect and admiration of the crowd as a means of attaining its own humanity. And so it leaps and ducks, turns and twirls, all to the rhythm of the unseen orchestra, and yet unfortunately, it goes unnoticed.

A child in the crowd watches the dancer, occasionally darting to the phantom pinned beneath. The strange shapes and curls of the eager spectre alter the granted glances and in a few moments, are changed from ephemeral to an insatiable fixation to the beauty of the minor soul trapped in the canvas on the floor.

As the performance mounts to a climax, the child’s attention deserts the brilliant dancer to the veneration of all the other attendees in the hall, focusing entirely on the shadow, completely impressed with the complex patterns it makes. In the child’s mind, it is the dancer that complements the shade and cannot be without it. The concert arrives at its completion, the dance comes to a momentous halt, and the lights come on, forcing the penumbra into obscurity in the glare of the bright, now well-lit, theatre.

Many years from now, in a place a long distance from here, a grown man recalls the vision of that child, the wonder and joy instilled in him by the phantom that wished so much to be free, as he leaps and dances, his shadow beneath him.
