"Sometimes, when the day reaches an arduous peak, I walk away. Retreating to the staircase on the side of the office, the secluded corner's flickering light provides the solace of silence much needed to unload. The assaulting barrage of calls and disturbing outsiders are barred on the other side of the visible door, its invisible counterpart keeping the mobile phone's network signal at bay. I pull a cigarette pack from the inner pocket of my jacket, putting one end of an unlit cigarette in my mouth and lighting it.

The euphoria lasts a few seconds, bringing with it a stream of clarity that lasts for the duration.

My mind starts to race and like flipping through a picture book, I begin to flick through a string of thoughts and memories, gaining momentum. Like a rushing roller coaster passing through a long, straight tunnel, I can see the end growing brighter until I finally step into the gauzy light that falls upon me in diaphanous folds. I bask in the artificial sun for moments more while strengthening my resolve to find a way, some way, to make it through the day. As the cigarette reaches its expected end, I visualize the butt catching fire and painlessly extending to my fingers and lips. As my face falls away in ashes and my fingers disintegrate, my body and clothes continue to burn away until there is nothing left but a pile of cinder.

Leaving my forlorn mood in the ashes behind me, I stub the cigarette out, straighten my back from its leaning posture and open the door to exit. 'A few hours more', I say. 'Just a few.'"

A pause of awkward silence ensues when I finish telling him this.

"Are you stoned?" he asks me.

Shaking my head I say earnestly, "No, why do you ask?"

He silently shrugs his shoulders.

"See you tomorrow?" I say, getting up from the pavement and dusting my trousers.

"Sure thing."

We shake hands and walk in separate directions...
