Mumbai, India 12th April, 2013  
Leopold Cafe 06:30 PM

Emptying my mind, the buzzing of the pulsating crowd floods my senses.  
Writing, offloading my emotions, casting away that which is dead or dying.  
Hollowing my mind by imagining an orchestra, stringed and wind instruments.  
Imagining a beautiful woman, speaking to me, captivating me with her smile.  
Thinking, painting a canvas in my head, vivid colors that hold my glance.  
Emptying my mind, my strength failing, the crowd washes over me.
