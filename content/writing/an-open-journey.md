A solitary tune carries me above the trembling winds, I know that it takes me to a place that I do not know. The accompanying sitar releases a bittersweet twang with every plucked string and I find myself stripped of a consciousness that was once my own, now embedded into a vast expanse of white, round, smooth pebbles and the gentle river that flows over, stretching from as far as I can see, to the innermost sanctums of my being.

The trees sway from side to side in the cool night air, whispering to each other a secret I desperately long to hear, a parable containing the combined wisdom of the farthest reaches of the universe. They begin to speak to me but hesitate, stilled by a sense of distrust as their leaves rustle, their haunting melody straining, as if attempting to lean over the shoulders of an obstructive crowd, spectators gathered to witness the capability of untested youth.

Raising my brow to the large, yellow moon, I whisper in response, "Wait for me", my voice muffled. "Be patient, old ones. My journey has only begun".
