Ladies and gentlemen. Ravi Shankar is no more.

The Pandit or Panditji, the Legend, the great musician, the master or maestro, and all the other bromidic terms to describe this great man, though clichéd and often overused, remained absolutely true to their meaning to his dying day, but are simply inapt at communicating the intensity and fervor I have felt for the music this man was capable of creating.

It would have to suffice to say that as the sentient entity trapped in the body of a man, wove music from the fabric of his soul with the instrumental tool, the sitar, I listened. An instant remedy to even my most insurmountable and arduous days, I heard him wail and cheer with my mind and heart.

Rest in Peace Ravi Shankar ji. I will miss you, old man.
