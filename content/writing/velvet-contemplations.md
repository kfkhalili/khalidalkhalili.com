The monotonous soliloquy giving shape to my thoughts leveled off into silence and in wordless contemplation, the back of my hand passed over her arms and abdomen in slow, continuous strides.

Unblemished and faultless, her skin is like velvet.

She fell asleep in the midst of one of my monologues, her head in my lap. Reflecting upon that moment, the few minutes of tranquil stillness would be too fleeting to grant the repose her body suddenly demanded, but were of an interval sufficient for me to sink into reverie. Incredulously attempting to recall to myself the happenings that brought about this specific moment, it became very evident that the reasons, though uncountable, were so precise that from here on forward the possibilities branch out infinitely, outlining to me the innumerable ways this story might end differently. Laden with events that are either susceptible to my influence or are completely beyond control, the conclusion would be an outcome of a multitude of actions, combined together into a blend that varies in intensity, timing, and sequence.

The responsibility of the part I would play weighed down heavily on me.

Her closed eyes then began to stir, displaying a movement indicative of deep sleep as she mouthed inaudible sentences in response to an unheard prompt.

She was dreaming.

My musings turned to imagine the nature of dreams induced by a position similar to the one we were in and beginning to wonder if they were of a pleasant sort, her momentarily furrowed brow steered my thoughts to a shadowy path. The anguished countenance presented for an instant provoked me to visualize the entrance to a dark and poorly ventilated part of a forest, its low hanging trees obstructing the cleansing force of light and wisdom, and its rampant vegetation stifling the free flow of air and consideration. A section of the mental map that was either uncharted or badly ministered.

Fueled by past sorrowful experiences, her pose was perhaps serving to amplify the ghosts emerging from the murky recesses of her mind. Maybe in time, this moment would be relegated to occupy that morose sector of her thoughts, to share it with other demons of a long gone age. Detecting an initially imperceptible shake in her arm and instinctively choosing to release her from it to the respite of the waking world, I ran my hand gently across her face and whispered her name softly as to not alarm her.

Her face relaxed and the corner of her mouth curved upwards in a gentle smile, dissolving the uninvited melancholic spectre that had enveloped me.

“You fell asleep.” I say to her while smiling, my hand enduring in its motion across her cheeks and temple.

“You have a relaxing effect on me.” She responds, her smile widening to reveal a set of pearly white teeth.

Her skin is like velvet.
