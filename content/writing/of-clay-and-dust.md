The universe unfolds endlessly in front of me. I was once part of a tumultuous star, descending from the vast expanse.

I am a man, made of clay. The present looking into the past.

Surviving since my inception on the mind of my ancestors. Armed with a repository of truth and deception, they say "Judge not, that ye be not judged".

The abdicating phantom guillotine falls upon moral responsibility.

Sequential time converts borrowed energy into thought and reason. I live for my own sake, my mind's verdicts and convictions. And until paths become inevitable and choices preordained it shall be spoken:

"Nay. Judge, and be judged the same. Fear not. A mind, unquenchable thirst for knowledge, concrete principals and a will of steel are all you will ever need. Hold these as a sword or serpent that in a moment would turn on you and your brothers. Keep them in place, the tools they are".

Light claims the ephemeral sleeper. My mind, now consumed, can rest in the ether. Scattered back into the never ending router of energy.

I am a man, made of dust. The present looking into the future.

We are the result, what's better left unspoken. The anvil that shatters the hammer.
